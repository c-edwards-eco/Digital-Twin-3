Collection Wall Virtual Shelves

This map currently displays the four floors of the collection wall located at the TU Delft Central Library.

The shelves were created with a matrix in Excel, and then transformed to a GeoJSON using R.

Faculty Expo areas are shaded by default, but catalogue data must be manually uploaded to visualize book placement. Catalogue data is stored temporarily in the browser.

Once catalogue data has been uploaded, you can explore the call number range for each bookcase, or search for a specific call number to find it or its closest match.

The purpose of this map is to visualize the future (re)arrangements of the iconic collection wall.
