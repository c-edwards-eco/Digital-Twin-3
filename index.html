Collection Wall Virtual Shelves

This map displays the four floors of the collection wall located at the TU Delft Central Library.

Faculty Expo areas are shaded by default, but catalogue data must be manually uploaded to visualize book placement. Catalogue data is stored temporarily in the browser.

Once catalogue data has been uploaded, you can explore the call number range for each bookcase, or search for a specific call number to find it or its closest match.

Clicking on a bookcase allows you to explore its contents. Hovering on a book will display its call number and title.

Books shown in bookcases are representative of the full range of titles within that bookcase and do not necessarily reflect the precise location of that book within the bookcase.
